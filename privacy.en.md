# Privacy notice

Kilmeh is an open benchmark that tests how well AI coding assistants handle Arabic. This page collects Arabic and Arabizi spellings of English words from native speakers. This notice explains what we collect and what we do with it.

## What we collect

- The country whose Arabic you speak (required; not where you live), or your own description if you choose Other. We derive a dialect group from it, for example Levantine for Jordan.
- The words you write: Arabic spellings, Arabizi spellings, feminine forms, and whether you keep a word in English.
- Your name (optional). Give it only if you want to be credited.
- Your email (optional). Give it only if you want us to be able to contact you.
- A random deletion code and a random contributor number, made by your browser.
- The time you sent your words, and the version of this notice you agreed to.

## Why

- To build an open benchmark that measures how well AI tools handle Arabic in many dialects.
- To credit contributors by name, but only those who tick "Credit me by name".
- To contact you about your contribution, but only if you gave an email.

## What is published

- The words you write are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0), together with that country and dialect group.
- Your name is published only if you tick "Credit me by name". It is off by default.
- Your email is never published, sold or shared.
- Your deletion code is never published.

## Where it is stored

Your answers are stored in a Supabase database. The public page can only add new rows: it cannot read, change or delete anything. Only the Kilmeh maintainers can read the data.

## How long we keep it

- Published words stay in the benchmark data while the project exists, unless you ask us to delete them.
- Names and emails are kept only as long as the project needs them to credit or contact contributors, and are deleted when the project ends.

## Deleting your data

After you send your words, the page shows a deletion code (for example K-ABCDE-23456). Keep it. To delete your entries, email the code to CONTACT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER and we will remove your entries and your contributor record. If you gave an email address, you can also write from that address instead of sending the code. Words that were already included in a published release cannot be removed from copies other people downloaded, but we will remove them from future releases.

## No tracking

- No cookies.
- No analytics, ads or tracking scripts.
- No third-party fonts or scripts.
- Your progress is kept in your browser's session storage so a refresh does not lose it. It is cleared when you close the tab. Your name and email are removed from it once your words are sent.
